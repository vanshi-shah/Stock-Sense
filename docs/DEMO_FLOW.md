# Demo Flow (StockSense)

1. **Authentication:** 
   - User lands on the Login/Signup screen.
   - Enters credentials and logs in.

2. **Dashboard Overview:**
   - User lands on the central Dashboard.
   - Views summary widgets for "Receipts", "Delivery", and other KPIs.

3. **Setup (Warehouses & Locations):**
   - Navigate to Warehouse setup to create a new warehouse.
   - Navigate to Location setup to create racks/shelves within the warehouse.

4. **Inbound Operations (Receipts):**
   - Navigate to Receipts List View.
   - Click "New Receipt" to enter the Detailed View.
   - Add a Vendor and products.
   - Click "Validate" to confirm stock arrival.

5. **Outbound Operations (Deliveries):**
   - Navigate to Delivery List View.
   - Click "New Delivery" to enter Detailed View.
   - Add a Customer and select products to dispatch.
   - Click "Validate" to deduct stock.

6. **Visibility & Tracking:**
   - Navigate to "Stock" to view current inventory levels across products.
   - Navigate to "Move History" to see the immutable ledger of the transactions just performed.
